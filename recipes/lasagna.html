A carefully prepared Lasagna for your satisfaction

A traditional lasagna recipe involves layering wide, flat pasta sheets with a rich meat sauce (ragù), a creamy cheese mixture, and mozzarella, then baking until golden and bubbly.

While traditional recipes use ricotta or cottage cheese, some variations include a béchamel sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lb ground beef and/or Italian sausage
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2–3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 (6 oz) can tomato paste
  • 1/2 cup water

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the ground beef and sausage, breaking it up with a spoon as you cook until browned.
  2. Stir in chopped onion and cook until softened, about 5 minutes. Add minced garlic and cook for 1 more minute until fragrant.
  3. Drain any excess fat.
  4. Add the crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, water, and seasonings (parsley, basil, oregano, salt, pepper, and sugar, if using).
  5. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer, covered, for at least 30 minutes to allow the flavors to meld.
